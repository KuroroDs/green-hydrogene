01 / About the academy

A place where research meets the future of energy.

H₂ Academy aims to make advanced hydrogen knowledge more accessible through education, scientific exploration and the sharing of technical resources. We bring together learners, researchers and energy enthusiasts to understand the full hydrogen value chain — from production and storage to transport and real-world applications.

Our mission is to support informed learning, encourage scientific curiosity and help develop the knowledge needed for the transition to cleaner energy systems.
